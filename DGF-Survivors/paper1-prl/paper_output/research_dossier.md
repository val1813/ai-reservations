# Research Dossier — PRL Quantum Information

## Target Journal: Physical Review Letters

### Key Constraints
- Max words: ~3750 total (abstract + body + captions + refs)
- Abstract: ≤120 words
- References: typically ≤30
- Figures: 4-6 typical
- No numbered section headings
- Superscript bracket citations [1-3]

### PRL Success Factors (from journal_configs/PRL.md)
1. Broad physics significance — not just one sub-field
2. Evidence strength matches claim strength
3. Clear novelty vs incremental contribution
4. Readable by non-specialists

### Common Rejection Reasons
1. Lacks broad physics significance
2. Claims too strong for evidence
3. Incremental rather than breakthrough

## Current Paper Assessment

### Strengths
- Exact "if and only if" theorem — rare in open quantum systems
- Corrects a published functional form (θ² ln(1/θ) vs θ⁴)
- Three testable predictions on existing IBM Q hardware
- S/N ratios >29 for all three tests
- Connects causal topology to quantum memory — cross-disciplinary appeal

### Potential Weaknesses (pre-Phase 2 assessment)
- Claims novelty of "causal topology as control parameter" needs precise literature positioning
- Experimental results are predicted not measured — reviewer will flag this
- The "discrepancy factor of 153" may read as overstated without actual hardware data
- Independent researcher (single author) — may trigger extra scrutiny
- SM mentions actual IBM Q experiments but paper only has predictions
- References [4,5] (Gangwar 2025, Buscemi 2025) need fetch-verification
- Reference [10] (Ferradini et al. 2025) needs precise content verification
