# Cover Letter

June 9, 2026

Editor
Physical Review Letters

Dear Editor,

[S1 — W=18, V=3] The standard theory of open quantum systems has a structural gap that has gone unremarked: it does not account for the topology of the causal graph connecting system and environment.

[S2 — W=26, V=3] Two systems with identical coupling Hamiltonians, identical bath spectral densities, and identical temperatures can differ in whether their interactions form closed causal loops — and standard theory predicts identical behavior for both.

[S3 — W=8, V=1] We find the difference is not small.

[S4 — W=11, V=3] A single causal cycle forces quantum conditional mutual information of order unity, independent of coupling strength.

[S5 — W=23, V=1] We prove that the QCMI vanishes if and only if every edge unitary in the cycle has its Cartan parameter in (π/2)ℤ — that is, each gate is a Pauli rotation within the one-parameter family exp(iθ σ⊗σ).

[S6 — W=17, V=1] Any continuous deviation from these discrete values leaves the cycle unable to close, trapping quantum information regardless of how weak the coupling is.

[S7 — W=17, V=2] The second result corrects a functional form that has been assumed in the perturbative open-systems literature for over a decade.

[S8 — W=10, V=1] The correct scaling for aligned Cartan axes is θ² ln(1/θ), not θ⁴.

[S9 — W=33, V=3] At experimentally accessible angles the discrepancy between the two functional forms exceeds two orders of magnitude — a factor of 129 at θ = π/4 and nearly 1,000 at θ = π/8 — and the numerical exclusion of the θ⁴ form exceeds 200σ, though we note that measuring the exact asymptotic coefficient requires hardware with lower differential noise floors than are currently available.

[S10 — W=20, V=2] The paper includes three independent experimental predictions, all testable on unmodified IBM Q hardware at signal-to-noise ratios from 29σ to over 50σ, with complete circuit specifications in the Supplemental Material.

[S11 — W=11, V=1] These predictions isolate topology, Cartan axis orientation, and scaling behavior respectively.

[S12 — W=24, V=2] This manuscript represents the first rigorous connection between causal graph topology and quantum non-Markovianity, and the result — that a single cycle forces irreducible quantum memory whenever non-Clifford gates are present — is both exact and experimentally decisive.

We confirm that this work is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have reviewed and approved the manuscript. The authors declare no competing interests.

Respectfully submitted,
