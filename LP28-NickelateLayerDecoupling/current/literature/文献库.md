### PRL 136, 216501 (2026)

**文件:** D:\文献\Physical Review Letters 2026\Issue #21\qgl1-zh9f.pdf  
**标题:** Electronic Layer Decoupling Driven by Density-Wave Order in La4Ni3O10  
**核心结果:** 偏振红外 spectroscopy 显示 La4Ni3O10 在 DW ordered phase 中 out-of-plane conductivity sharply suppressed；rho_c/rho_ab 从约 70 增长到约 2600。解释为 SDW-induced redistribution of Ni dz2 occupation within trilayers，导致 Ni-O layers effective electronic decoupling。伴随 out-of-plane phonon shifts/splitting。
