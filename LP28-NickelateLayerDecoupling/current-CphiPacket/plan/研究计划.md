# Research Plan: LP28-CphiPacket

## Objective

Decide whether the `R_oe` residual-collapse protocol can be executed with a real or near-real nickelate data packet.

## Required Packet

Minimum fields:

1. `P_oe` block:
   - `chi_odd`, `chi_even`;
   - qz/polarization/form-factor resolved normal-state susceptibility;
   - frozen temperature grid;
   - raw `S_channel -> chi_unit` calibration;
   - denominator floor and censoring.

2. `B_min` block:
   - oxygen stoichiometry / layer asymmetry;
   - strain and lattice geometry;
   - dz2 / rare-earth 5d / ligand-hole / self-doping proxies;
   - disorder and dephasing;
   - pressure and thermal path;
   - ordinary c-axis coherence;
   - sample quality.

3. leakage-control block:
   - no `Tc`, zero resistance, Meissner, diamagnetism, gap, spin resonance, critical current, or output-trained labels before residual call;
   - pre-frozen `U_miss`;
   - independent batch/sample/probe controls.

## Round 1 A/B Assignment

A博士:

Find the strongest feasible route to assemble the packet. Prefer concrete existing or near-existing data sources, proxy substitution rules, and same-sample/matched-specimen designs. Do not claim `R_oe` survives.

B博士:

Argue the packet is non-identifiable or practically impossible. Focus on same-source contamination, calibration coupling, pressure-path non-repeatability, oxygen/sample-quality confounding, and small-N impossibility. Do not use universal no-go rhetoric.

## Round 1 Decision Target

Produce one of:

- `PACKET_FEASIBLE_NOW`: existing data can be assembled with tolerable gaps.
- `PACKET_FEASIBLE_NEAR_FUTURE`: requires a new but realistic measurement campaign.
- `PACKET_NONIDENTIFIABLE`: `P_oe` and `B_min` cannot be separated cleanly.
- `PACKET_NOT_FOUND`: no enough rows/probes; protocol remains bounded only.

## Breakthrough Criterion

This becomes a real progress route only if it identifies a concrete executable packet or proves that the packet cannot be made without leakage. A literature survey alone is insufficient.
