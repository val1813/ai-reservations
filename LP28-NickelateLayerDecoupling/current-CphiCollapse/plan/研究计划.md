# Research Plan: LP28-CphiCollapse

## Objective

Determine whether `C_phi` can be a nontrivial input-side theoretical variable, or whether it is provably / empirically reducible to prior-art c-axis coherence and sample quality.

## Core A/B

A: Independent `C_phi` exists.

It should produce at least one pre-output observable rank or invariant that differs from all simpler baselines:

- total c-axis spectral weight
- Drude fraction alone
- disorder / oxygen vacancy / strain
- dephasing / scattering
- `dz2` spectral weight

B: Independent `C_phi` does not exist.

Every lab-feasible `C_phi` collapses to one or more of the above baselines under realistic sample counts and pressure/stoichiometry uncertainty.

## Success Condition

The window succeeds only if it produces one of:

1. a no-go theorem / strong argument showing `C_phi` collapses to baselines;
2. a concrete pre-output discriminator that cannot be reduced to known c-axis coherence or sample quality;
3. a falsifiable experimental design where A and B make opposite predictions before bulk output is opened.

## Failure Condition

If the work only improves P0 packaging, hashes, thresholds, or coefficient stability, it fails the user's stated goal. That would be protocol engineering, not theory progress.
