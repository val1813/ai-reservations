# Research Plan: LP28-S1

## Objective

Decide whether `I_phi` can be made into a pre-registered, input-side phase-bus readiness index rather than a posterior label for superconducting output.

## Candidate Index

Working form:

`I_phi = E_z2 * C_phi / (kB*T + hbar*Gamma_phi + E_floor)`

This form is provisional. A/B must attack the units, observability, circularity, and thresholding. It must not be treated as validated.

## Variables To Operationalize

- `E_z2`: input-side interlayer `dz2` channel energy or spectral-weight proxy.
- `C_phi`: coherence/connectivity factor for the recoverable interlayer phase bus.
- `Gamma_phi`: input-side dephasing or scattering scale, not inferred from bulk superconducting output.
- `E_floor`: disorder/structural floor preventing infinite index values in low-temperature limits.

## Round 1 Task

A博士: literature-driven route. Find whether similar non-circular input-side indices already exist, identify measurable quantities and fatal circularity risks, and give a concrete pre-registration protocol if possible.

B博士: nonstandard route. Borrow a mathematical structure from outside the standard interlayer-coherence framing to test whether the predictor/outcome split can be made sharper, ideally by information leakage, causal inference, or network observability.

## Hard Failure Conditions

- `I_phi` requires fitting to `Tc`, shielding, zero resistance, or bulk phase stiffness.
- the only available measurements are output-side superconducting observables.
- thresholds are chosen after seeing phase diagrams.
- the proposed index reduces to already-known interlayer coherence claims without a new non-circular testing protocol.

## Success Condition

S1 survives only if A/B converge on a measurable same-sample protocol where `I_phi` is fully specified before bulk output is inspected, with a clear go/no-go test and known failure modes.
