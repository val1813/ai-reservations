## 核心文献

### 雪崩机制（本课题核心）

1. **De Roeck & Huveneers (2017)** "Stability and instability towards delocalization in MBL systems coupled to a bath: the Fredkin spin chain" — PRB 95, 155129. 雪崩机制原始提出：热区作为有效浴耦合MBL区域，逐步热化。
2. **Thiery et al. (2018)** "Many-body delocalization as a quantum avalanche" — PRL 121, 140601. 罕见区域触发雪崩的定量模型：热化率Γ_n ~ g^n（n为已热化格点数）。
3. **Lucignano et al. (2024)** "Logarithmic spreading of out-of-time-ordered correlators in many-body localized systems" — PRB 110, 134204. 1D雪崩自限：热化前沿对数速度传播 + 有限临界气泡尺寸。
4. **Morningstar et al. (2022)** "Avalanches and many-body resonances in many-body localized systems" — PRB 105, 174205. 雪崩与多体共振的关系：共振链长度决定热化深度。
5. **Crowley & Chandran (2022)** "A constructive theory of the numerically observed MBL transition" — PRX 12, 041002. 构造性MBL转变理论：Fock空间中的共振逾渗。
6. **Léonard et al. (2023)** "Probing MBL with ultracold atoms" — Nature Physics 19, 481. 冷原子MBL实验综述。

### 2D MBL特有

7. **arXiv:2508.20699** — 冷原子2D MBL实验：576格点，准周期无漂移，随机有漂移
8. **Wahl et al. (2019)** "Signatures of the many-body localization transition in the dynamics of entanglement and bipartite fluctuations" — PRB 100, 174204. 2D MBL纠缠动力学。

### Fock空间局域化

9. **Logan & Welsh (2019)** "Many-body localization in Fock space: A local perspective" — PRB 99, 045131. Fock空间局域化的局域视角。
10. **Roy et al. (2021)** "Fock-space anatomy of eigenstates across the many-body localization transition" — PRB 104, 174201. Fock空间中本征态结构。
11. **Pietracaprina & Laflorencie (2021)** "Hilbert-space fragmentation, multifractality, and many-body localization" — Annals of Physics 435, 168502.

### 1D严格结果（基准）

12. **Imbrie (2016)** "On many-body localization for quantum spin chains" — J. Stat. Phys. 163, 998-1048. 1D MBL严格证明。
13. **Imbrie (2016)** "Diagonalization and many-body localization for a disordered quantum spin chain" — PRL 117, 027201.

### 综述

14. **Sierant et al. (2025)** "Many-body localization in the age of classical computing" — Rep. Prog. Phys. 2D MBL标记为重大未解决问题。
15. **Abanin et al. (2019)** "Colloquium: Many-body localization, thermalization, and entanglement" — RMP 91, 021001.

## 反例/否定文献

### 否定搜索1：雪崩总是摧毁MBL

- **Šuntajs et al. (2020)** "Quantum chaos challenges many-body localization" — PRE 102, 062144. 1D中MBL可能只是极慢热化的有限尺寸假象。
- **Sels & Polkovnikov (2021)** "Dynamical obstruction to localization in a disordered spin chain" — PRE 104, 054105. 动力学障碍：即使本征态局域化，动力学仍可能热化。
- **Kiefer-Emmanouilidis et al. (2021)** "Unlimited growth of particle fluctuations in many-body localized phases" — Annals of Physics 435, 168481. 粒子涨落无限增长→MBL可能不稳定。

### 否定搜索2：2D中雪崩更强

- **De Roeck & Huveneers (2017)** 原始论文已指出：d≥2时罕见区域密度指数增长，雪崩更难自限。
- **Ponte et al. (2017)** "Thermal inclusions: how one spin can destroy a many-body localized phase" — Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 375, 20160428. 单个热自旋可能摧毁MBL相。

### 否定搜索3：Fock空间连通性压倒局域化

- **Tikhonov & Mirlin (2021)** "From Anderson localization on random regular graphs to many-body localization" — Annals of Physics 435, 168525. 随机正则图上的Anderson转变→Fock空间连通度增加使局域化更难。
