## Observations

### Observation 1: Strong recency
Four of eight references (50%) are from 2024–2025, which is appropriate for a rapidly moving field. Ref [2] (2022) is the most recent review and is the standard entry point. The classic reviews [1,3,4] provide necessary background.

### Observation 2: Author overlap (coherent research community)
Refs [5] and [7] share author B. K. Agarwalla, and all three Phys. Rev. B papers [5,7,8] study variants of the same system (boundary-driven fermion chains with dephasing). This is a coherent, tightly-connected research community — no suspicious citation patterns detected.

### Observation 3: Content attribution to Ref [8]
The manuscript states that Bhat & Žnidarič [8] "proved a structural theorem for nearest-neighbor XX chains: the off-diagonal elements of the single-particle correlation matrix are purely imaginary, with Im(C_{j,j+1}) directly yielding the magnetization current." The verified title of Ref [8] is "Transfer matrix approach to quantum systems subject to certain Lindblad evolution." While the paper does analyze the XX chain with dephasing and the correlation matrix C_{jk} (including the imaginary off-diagonal structure), the paper's main contribution is a transfer matrix solution method. The "structural theorem" about pure imaginary off-diagonals may be one result within the paper rather than its primary theorem. This is not a reference authenticity issue, but the manuscript's characterization of what Ref [8] "proved" may overstate or mischaracterize the paper's main result. **Recommendation:** Verify that the "purely imaginary off-diagonal" result is explicitly proven in Ref [8], or adjust the phrasing to more accurately describe the paper's contribution.

### Observation 4: Ref [5] — μ(α) valid range
The manuscript correctly notes that μ(α) = 2α − 2 is valid for 1 < α < 3/2 and that values for α > 3/2 in Table 2 are labeled as "extrapolated." This is an honest representation. Dhawan et al. [5] indeed derive this relation in the superdiffusive regime (1 < α < 1.5) with crossover to diffusive at α ≈ 1.5.

### Observation 5: All arXiv versions verify
Ref [6] is correctly cited as v3 (arXiv:2504.00188v3). The arXiv page confirms three versions exist, and the paper was submitted 31 March 2025.
