Please consider the manuscript "Macrocosmic Decoherence as Archive-Decompression Dynamics" for publication in Physical Review Letters.

The manuscript revises DGF around a distinction between flowing quantum information and archived classical information.  The accessible coherence variable is \(q=N_{\rm accessible}/N_{\rm total}(V)\); decoherence is \(q\to0\).  Mass is assigned to the archived fraction \(A=1-q\), resolving the apparent contradiction that ordinary decoherence does not reduce rest mass.

The main chain is: local Planck-cell access weights define \(q\); receiver capacity drives a Fokker-Planck drift toward smaller \(q\); the archived fraction \(A\) gives the information-geometric angle \(\theta=\pi A\); the Euler residual yields \(m(A)=2m_p\sin(\pi A/2)/\pi\); and receiver modes enter the decoherence kernel through \(J_{\rm recv}(\omega)\), whose Markovian limit recovers the GUP \(p^4/3m_S\) kernel.

The larger conjecture is an archive-decompression cycle: once local archived information reaches a Planck critical density, the region resets toward quantum accessibility.  This is included as an open prediction, not as the main proven theorem.
