# DGF v3.1-prd Downgrade Notes

Date: 2026-06-05

## Purpose

The paper was downgraded from a PRL/Nature-Physics-style "unified theory"
claim to a PRD/foundations-style effective framework.  The goal is not to
make the paper publication-ready, but to remove desk-reject triggers caused by
overstated derivations and unsupported quantitative predictions.

## Main edits

1. Title changed from a unified-theory framing to:

   `An Effective Information-Geometry Framework for Decoherence, Metric Representations, and Gravitational Interfaces`

2. Abstract now states that DGF is an effective information-geometric program,
   not a completed derivation of spacetime, mass, or gravity.

3. A new `Status and Scope of This Draft` section was added near the start.
   It front-loads four limitations:

   - coherent quantum probes do not imply order-unity gravity suppression;
   - the linear point-source solution does not screen cosmological `Gdot/G`;
   - `G` fixes only `q_inf l^2`, so `q_inf` is not absolutely identifiable;
   - graph RG should be a 3D isotropic universality-class statement, not
     microscopic `Z^3` uniqueness.

4. `Falsifiable Predictions` was renamed to `Conditional Tests and Consistency
   Checks`.

5. Prediction 1 was rewritten as a conditional source-state gravity
   modification.  The coherent-probe interpretation is explicitly excluded.

6. The conclusion now says DGF v3.1-prd is an auditable effective language and
   does not establish a new fundamental theory of spacetime, mass, or gravity.

## Reviewer warning retained

The remaining PRD-level risk is that the framework may still lack a hard,
normalization-independent discriminator.  A future version needs at least one
test that cannot be absorbed into:

- free normalization of `q_inf`;
- a posterior choice of `q-gradient -> acceleration` interface;
- a conditional source/test/environment coupling function.

Without such a discriminator, the paper is an organized parametrization rather
than a theory result.
