# 文献库

## 核心文献

1. Jankousky et al., "Effective bands and band-like electron transport in amorphous solids", *Nature Physics* 22, 88-93 (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41567-025-03099-x.
   - 用 a-In2O3 展示 effective bands 和 band-like transport。
   - 关键参数: m*≈0.2m0, ΔE≈0.25 eV, μ≈13 cm2/Vs, mean free path 1-3 nm。
   - 机制线索: In-O 局域配位、edge/corner-sharing connectivity、extended In-s orbital、O-O 缺陷。

2. Lee, Cobb, Dodabalapur, "Band transport and mobility edge in amorphous solution-processed zinc tin oxide thin-film transistors", *Applied Physics Letters* 97 (2010). DOI: 10.1063/1.3517502.
   - 非晶氧化物中 band transport/mobility edge 的先例。

3. Stern, "Band-Tail Model for Optical Absorption and for the Mobility Edge in Amorphous Silicon", *Physical Review B* 3 (1971). DOI: 10.1103/physrevb.3.2636.
   - mobility-edge/band-tail 老框架。

4. Aliano, Catellani, Cicero, "Characterization of amorphous In2O3: An ab initio molecular dynamics study", *Applied Physics Letters* 99 (2011). DOI: 10.1063/1.3664224.
   - a-In2O3 结构无序、局域态、O-O bond 先例。

5. Furubayashi, Maehara, Yamamoto, "New Insights on Factors Limiting the Carrier Transport in Very Thin Amorphous Sn-Doped In2O3 Films with High Hall Mobility", *Nanoscale Research Letters* 14 (2019). DOI: 10.1186/s11671-019-2948-4.
   - a-ITO Hall mobility 和输运限制因素。

6. Jang et al., "InGaZnO4 and In2O3 Mobility Trend: The Role of Structural Disorder from Amorphous to Crystalline States", *ACS Materials Letters* (2025). DOI: 10.1021/acsmaterialslett.5c00348.
   - 结构无序到迁移率趋势的数据来源候选。

## 先发拦截结论

不能声称以下内容为新:

- 非晶氧化物可有 band-like transport。
- mobility edge 可解释非晶半导体/非晶氧化物带边输运。
- s-like conduction band 有利于 amorphous oxide semiconductor 高迁移率。

LP29 的差异化只能放在:

- same-sample `k_F l` / `E_F-E_c` 与 In-s connectivity/O-O defect graph 的判据比较；
- matched carrier density 和 matched `E_F-E_c` 下的网络阈值是否仍独立预测 spectral ridge/IPR/level statistics/μ_H 崩塌。
