# REVIEWER_NSF_FCS2T_final

Role: independent REVIEWER for NSF-FCS-2T, Nature Physics hostile stance.

Reviewed gate claim only:

> In the tested finite-volume long-jump open-exclusion ledger, `R2=lambda_T''-2G_T` acts as a parity-tomography diagnostic: centered density bias contributes an even `delta^2` channel, equal-density nonLDB site skew contributes an even `A^2` channel, the signed mixed `delta A` channel is numerical-floor in the tested window, and reversible LDB traffic preserves equilibrium FDT while renormalizing density-biased NESS coefficients.

## 0. Hallucination / Dimensional / Direction / Circularity / Magnitude Checks

Dimensional convention:

- `R2=lambda_T''-2G_T` is dimensionally admissible only inside the project's dimensionless Markov-affinity ledger convention where `lambda_T''` and `G_T` share the FDT-normalized ledger unit.
- `R2/G_T = c0 + c_delta2 delta^2 + c_A2 A^2 + c_deltaA delta A + c_delta2A2 delta^2 A^2` is dimensionless if `delta` and `A` are dimensionless perturbation coordinates. No SI-level physical unit statement survives outside that convention.

Direction/sign checks:

- Centered density bias: local CSV fit has `c_delta2>0` in all checked `L=4..7`, `alpha=0.5,1.0`; evenness under `delta -> -delta` is consistent with the signed basis.
- nonLDB site skew: local CSV fit has `c_A2=0.0038419818800062705..0.013000641841500404` and `c_deltaA` floor-scale, with max absolute `|c_deltaA|=1.2465732135294796e-7`.
- reversible side: local CSV fit has floor-scale `c_A2` but sizable `c_delta2A2=0.009629772438973196..0.052507780981617055`, so it is not a separability null.

Circularity check:

- The signed fit did not first divide out `delta A`; it used signed grids over `delta=0,+/-0.02,+/-0.04` and `A=0,+/-0.05,+/-0.10`. That avoids the earlier obvious circularity failure.
- Remaining circularity risk: all evidence is generated by the same finite-volume ledger implementation and fit ansatz. It validates internal numerical bookkeeping, not an analytic Markov-generator symmetry theorem.

Magnitude check:

- nonLDB `c_deltaA` is about 5-6 orders below `c_A2` and about 6 orders below `c_delta2`.
- reversible `rel_resid=0.05857635982337634..0.1371259092726748` in the minimal signed basis is not numerical floor; it is a warning that reversible traffic produces real higher mixed structure in NESS.
- No >10-order unexplained numerical gulf was found inside the active finite-window claim, but the floor claim depends on solver precision and conditioning of a small grid.

## 2. Five Review Reasons

1. The claim is scientifically underpowered because it is finite-window bookkeeping without an analytic generator-symmetry derivation. The manuscript would ask the reader to infer parity structure from `L=4..7`, two `alpha` values, and a small signed perturbation grid. A Nature Physics-level claim needs a proof or at least a parameter-independent structural argument for why `delta`, `A`, and `delta A` vanish in the stated sector. [严重]

2. The central vocabulary, "parity tomography", is not yet a physical observable or invariant; it is a fitted basis decomposition of `R2/G_T`. Without a definition stable under alternative normalizations, different counting-field conventions, different fit bases, and changes in the perturbation window, this is a diagnostic label rather than a result. The author must prove convention robustness or rename it as a local numerical fit diagnostic. [严重]

3. The standard nonequilibrium response literature substantially covers the mechanism. Baiesi-Maes-Wynants and related frenetic-response work already separate entropic and time-symmetric traffic/activity contributions in Markov dynamics; MFT and open-exclusion FCS already cover current cumulants and fluctuation symmetries. The present claim has not isolated what is new beyond applying those frameworks to one finite long-jump ledger. [致命]

4. The reversible-side result weakens the separability story. The same signed fit that shows equilibrium floor-scale behavior gives `c_delta2A2=0.0096..0.0525` and relative residuals up to about `0.137` for reversible traffic. Calling this "preserves equilibrium FDT while renormalizing NESS coefficients" is acceptable only as a caveat; it cannot support a clean tomography narrative unless the reversible sector is analytically separated from nonLDB skew. [中等]

5. Numerical-floor claims are not precision claims. The reported `c_deltaA=O(1e-7)` may reflect solver tolerance, finite-difference conditioning, basis collinearity, or cancellation in a centered protocol. The current files do not show precision sweeps, alternative fit bases, larger/smaller `delta,A` windows, or independent implementations. The floor statement is therefore empirical and local, not a resolved absence. [中等]
