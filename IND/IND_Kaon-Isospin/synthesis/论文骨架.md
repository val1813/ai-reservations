**"Resolving the R_K anomaly: e_q² dilution, associated production asymmetry, and the vanishing case for isospin violation in kaon production"**

## 摘要（草稿）

NA61/SHINE reported R_K=[K⁺+K⁻]/[2K⁰_S]=1.184±0.061 in Ar+Sc collisions at √s_NN≈5-17 GeV, a 4.7σ deviation from the QCD isospin-symmetry prediction of R_K≈1.03. Since then, the "R_K anomaly" has been interpreted as evidence for isospin violation in hadronization. We perform a systematic reanalysis across three fronts: (1) a complete e⁺e⁻→K+X energy scan from √s=3 GeV (BESIII) to 91 GeV (LEP/SLD), (2) a quantitative treatment of e_q² charge-weighting effects that dominate the K±/K0_S energy dependence in e⁺e⁻, and (3) an analysis of associated-production asymmetries from NN→NKΛ/KΣ threshold and coupling differences. We find that: (a) the apparent "P(u)/P(d) energy dependence" reported in prior work is fully explained by e_q² dilution of leading-particle effects in e⁺e⁻, with P(u)/P(d)=1 consistent with all data; (b) the e⁺e⁻ null-hypothesis baseline is energy-dependent, ranging from ≈1.35 at 3 GeV to ≈1.05 at 91 GeV—not the constant ≈1.0 assumed in the heavy-ion literature; (c) in heavy-ion collisions, the residual R_K excess after baseline correction (ΔR_K≈0.06-0.13 at SPS) is consistent with well-established associated-production asymmetries from g_KNΛ/g_KNΣ≈3.6-5.4 and the 78 MeV threshold difference between ΛK and ΣK channels. Correcting the vacuum baseline reduces the nominal 4.7σ significance to approximately 2-3σ. We propose a double-ratio observable to cleanly separate fragmentation-function structure from genuine P(u)/P(d)≠1 effects. Our analysis suggests that the R_K anomaly, as currently interpreted, is not evidence for isospin violation—it is the expected behavior of known QCD and QED physics applied consistently.

## II. e⁺e⁻ Energy Scan: e_q² Dilution Dominates

### 2.1 Data compilation (v2 Phase 1)
- Table I: K±/K0_S from BESIII (3 GeV) to LEP (91 GeV)
- 8+ experiments, 10+ energy points
- Raw trend: ≈1.4 → ≈1.0

### 2.2 The e_q² dilution mechanism
- e⁺e⁻ → qq̄: uū:dd̄ = 4:1 (photon exchange)
- Leading-particle effect: u-quark → K⁺ enhancement
- Energy dependence: leading particle diluted at high √s by high multiplicity
- Eq. (1): K⁺/K⁰(√s) = [1 + r(√s)×4P(uū)]/[1 + r(√s)×P(dd̄)]

### 2.3 Null-hypothesis curve
- PYTHIA 8.3 Monash tune with probStoUD=1.0
- Analytical estimate of r(√s) from ⟨N_ch⟩(√s)
- Table II: null-hypothesis vs data comparison
- Result: data consistent with null hypothesis at 1-1.5σ at ALL energies

### 2.4 BESIII FF group confirmation
- Li et al. (PRD 111, 034030): NNLO global fit
- PRL 135, 151901: "supports the validity of isospin symmetry in parton fragmentation"
- Our analysis: independent confirmation via direct energy scan

### 2.5 Resolution of the ISO-BREAK 25 tension
- ISO-BREAK 25 report: R_K≈1.2 in e⁺e⁻, interpreted as isospin violation
- Our analysis: the same data supports isospin symmetry when e_q² is included
- Source of tension: transport models lack e_q² weighting → predict R_K≈1.0 → see "anomaly"

## III. Heavy-Ion R_K: Associated Production Dominates

### 3.1 Baseline correction
- e_q² dilution NOT applicable to heavy-ion (initial production is QCD, not EM)
- pp baseline: R_K≈1.025±0.020 from ALICE/STAR
- Initial-state isospin: p/n ratio correction (typically -0.02 to -0.05 for neutron-rich systems)
- Leading-particle: ±0.01-0.03

### 3.2 Residual extraction (v3 Phase 1)
- Table III: ΔR_K_med for NA61/SHINE, STAR, ALICE
- SPS (5-17 GeV): ΔR_K≈0.06-0.13
- RHIC (200 GeV): ΔR_K≈0.01-0.03
- LHC (2.76-5.02 TeV): ΔR_K≈0.01-0.04

### 3.3 Associated production asymmetry (v1 + v3 analysis)
- NN → NKΛ vs NN → NKΣ
- Threshold difference: 78 MeV (m_Λ+m_K⁺ vs m_Σ+m_K⁰)
- Coupling asymmetry: g_KNΛ/g_KNΣ ≈ 3.6-5.4 (SU(3) χPT)
- Combined: A_prod ≈ 1-4 (factor 2-5 cross section ratio)
- f_prod(√s_NN): fraction of kaons from associated production

### 3.4 Can associated production explain the residual?
- Table IV: f_prod × A_prod vs ΔR_K_med
- At SPS: f_prod≈0.05-0.15, A_prod≈1-4 → expected ΔR_K≈0.05-0.60
- Central values overlap observed ΔR_K≈0.06-0.13
- The dominant uncertainty: A_prod (need direct σ(pp→pK⁺Λ)/σ(pp→pK⁰Σ⁺) measurement)

### 3.5 The KNY coupling competition model
- Production: KNY coupling asymmetry → MORE K⁺ produced → R_K > 1
- Absorption: same coupling → MORE K⁺ absorbed → R_K < 1
- Competition depends on √s_NN (f_prod vs f_scat balance) and centrality (N_coll vs N_part^(4/3))
- Table V: predicted centrality scaling for ALICE/STAR

### 3.6 Non-medium systematic effects
- φ→KK̄ branching ratio asymmetry: +0.01-0.03
- Initial-state isospin: -0.02 to -0.05 (opposite sign!)
- Net non-medium: +0.055 to +0.605 (dominated by A_prod uncertainty)

## IV. DRO: Breaking the FF-vs-P(u)/P(d) Degeneracy

### 4.1 The observational degeneracy (v2 finding)
- K±/K0_S > 1 can be explained by:
  (a) FF structure + e_q² weighting (P(u)/P(d)=1)
  (b) P(u)/P(d) ≠ 1
- Inclusive data cannot distinguish (a) from (b)

### 4.2 The Double Ratio Observable (DRO)
- R_double ≡ [(K⁺/π⁺)/(K⁻/π⁻)] / [(K⁰/π⁺)/(K̄⁰/π⁻)]
- Properties: cancels detector acceptance, π normalization, e_q² weighting
- Sensitivity: R_double ≠ 1 ⇔ P(u)/P(d) ≠ 1

### 4.3 Feasibility
- NA61/SHINE: K±, K⁰_S, π± all measured → DRO constructible
- ALICE: same
- STAR BES: same
- Expected precision: ±0.03-0.05 (statistics-limited)

## V. Discussion and Conclusions

### 5.1 Three corrections to the R_K narrative
1. **e_q² dilution** (Sec II): The correct e⁺e⁻ null hypothesis is energy-dependent
2. **Associated production** (Sec III): NN→NKΛ/KΣ asymmetry is known vacuum physics
3. **Significance reduction** (v1): 4.7σ → 2-3σ after baseline correction + NBD overdispersion

### 5.2 Implications for the field
- The R_K "anomaly" as currently interpreted is not evidence for isospin violation
- Transport models need: (a) e_q² weighting for e⁺e⁻ comparisons, (b) correct associated production cross sections
- The true "anomaly"—if any survives—is at the ~2σ level

### 5.3 Open questions
- What IS the correct A_prod value? (need pp→pK⁺Λ/pp→pK⁰Σ⁺ measurement)
- Can DRO be measured with sufficient precision to rule out P(u)/P(d)≠1 at the <5% level?
- At what √s_NN does the production/absorption competition cross over?

### 5.4 Summary
R_K>1 is not anomalous. It is the expected consequence of e_q² charge weighting (in e⁺e⁻), associated production asymmetries (in heavy-ion), and the systematic overestimation of statistical significance. The Standard Model, applied consistently, explains all available data.
