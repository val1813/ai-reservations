# 导航员报告 — v4-deepseek → v5

## 课题总结一句话
Strong-coupling quantum thermodynamics has built a gauge-invariant mathematical edifice whose proof foundations assume conditions (initial factorization, infinite bath) that are systematically violated in the strong-coupling regime the edifice was designed for.

## 候选北极星

### 候选1（推荐优先） — ⭐⭐极高潜力 (M6+T6)
北极星：**Close the Spohn gap — extend gauge-invariant entropy production Σ_S^{(gauge)} to correlated initial states, or determine precisely what is lost when the factorization premise fails in the strong-coupling regime.**

驱动矛盾：Proposition A: v4's gauge-invariant entropy production Σ_S^{(gauge)} ≥ 0 relies on Spohn's proof that -d/dt S(ρ_S || ρ_S^eq) ≥ 0. Proposition B: Spohn's proof assumes ρ(0)=ρ_S⊗ρ_B (no initial S-B correlations). Proposition C: In the strong-coupling regime where gauge ambiguity is consequential, system-bath correlations are ubiquitous and the initial product-state assumption is physically unrealistic. These three cannot all be satisfied — the formula is either unproven in its target regime, or the target regime must be restricted to experimentally inaccessible initial conditions.

客观性判定：Any researcher building a thermodynamic framework on Spohn's relative entropy production, regardless of method (HMF, reaction coordinate, polaron), faces: "When initial correlations exist, what does my entropy production formula actually measure?" Spohn (1978) has 47 years of citation history; the factorization assumption has been noted as a limitation but never systematically confronted for gauge-invariant entropy production.

可及性：HIGH. The next step — replacing the factorization assumption with an information-theoretic correction using quantum mutual information I(S:B) — has clear mathematical handles. Resource theory of correlated states already treats initial correlations as a resource. 90% of tools exist.
