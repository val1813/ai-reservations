1. Gough showed SFR tracks DESI CPL parameters — but lacked a microscopic mechanism.
2. We propose: dark energy emerges from a cosmic qubit ensemble — each gravitational collapse event determines a qubit from |0⟩ to |1⟩, consuming information capacity and modifying the vacuum energy.

4. a_c² is derived from mean-field theory, not fitted. The driving function is the gravitational collapse power density, computed from the halo model with zero phenomenological inputs.
5. Predicted w(z) is consistent with DESI DR2 binned constraints (Δχ² ≈ +5 over ΛCDM, indicative). An analog quantum simulation on 3-5 qubit chips is proposed.
